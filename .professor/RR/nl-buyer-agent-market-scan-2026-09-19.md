# RR — Dutch home-buying market scan for an AI buyer-agent app: Roofmatch pricing, the pre-Funda window, competitor-or-vendor, and whether makelaars accept bot viewing requests

Question: answer four questions about the Dutch home-BUYING market for a founder designing an AI buyer-agent app (AI interviews buyer -> scans listings -> researches -> asks "book a viewing?" -> books into the makelaar's calendar). (1) Roofmatch (roofmatch.com) charges no-cure-no-pay EUR 2,599 / 3,599 — what EXACTLY does the buyer get for that, per tier, and how does it compare with typical aankoopmakelaar fees and cheaper online/hybrid services? (2) Roof Radar (roofradar.app) claims listings 24-36 hours before Funda — HOW? (3) Would Roofmatch/Roof Radar be a COMPETITOR or a possible VENDOR/partner — API, white-label, B2B, affiliate? Same for other pre-Funda sources. (4) Are selling makelaars WILLING to accept viewing requests arriving from an automated agent/bot or third-party platform on a buyer's behalf?

## Answer

**The rulebook is on your side but the plumbing is not.** The NVM Erecode annex says anyone may view a publicly advertised home and that having an aankoopmakelaar may not by itself be a selection criterion — so a buyer-side agent is not excluded in principle; but there is no legitimate machine door: no open NL listing API, Funda's own terms expressly forbid scraping, data mining and "use to train (generative) artificial intelligence", and the only externally usable booking path found is a shareable Realworks Bezichtigingsplanner URL, not an API. On the competitive map, **Roofmatch is a direct competitor with no B2B surface at all (every partner/API path 404s), while Roof Radar is a credible vendor/partner — it already sells a white-label version of its alerts to makelaars.**

## Load-bearing facts

### Q1 — What Roofmatch actually delivers per tier

Pricing is a **EUR 299 non-refundable start fee (currently promotionally waived) plus a no-cure-no-pay success fee** payable only on completion ([roofmatch.com/en](https://www.roofmatch.com/en), [city page](https://www.roofmatch.com/en/aankoopmakelaar/amsterdam)).

**Both tiers include** a dedicated human buying agent plus AI search bots giving 24/7 alerts on pre-Funda and off-market listings; unlimited AI-driven valuation and bid-price advice (waardebepaling/biedstrategie); bid submission and negotiation with the selling agent by the agent; bouwkundige keuring coordination; koopovereenkomst review; and roughly two months of post-purchase Q&A.

- **Aankoop Pro — EUR 2,599.** The buyer does their own viewings: the site explicitly states the buyer attends personally since a selling agent is already present. *Two fetched Roofmatch pages disagree on whether Pro's guidance runs through to key transfer or stops at the winning bid — unverified.*
- **Aankoop Pro+ — EUR 3,599.** Everything in Pro, plus guidance from intake through full completion, faster/priority viewing-booking assistance, deeper contract review, notary/akte van levering guidance, and a final check before key handover (eindinspectie).
- **Not mentioned anywhere on the fetched pages:** ontbindende voorwaarden / voorbehoud financiering handling, and any NVM, VBO or Vastgoedpro membership claim. Both are absent rather than denied — treat as unverified, not as "excluded".
- A real human agent is assigned per client; AI is positioned as support (search bots, valuation and bid-strategy engines), not a replacement.
- Operator is **Roofmatch International B.V.**, Sassenheim, SBI 63910 (web portal activities), operational director van Galen K. listed from 2025 ([company.info](https://companyinfo.nl/organisatieprofiel/activiteiten-van-webportalen/roofmatch-international-b-v-sassenheim-99438836-000064509834)); the site names founders Stef (CEO), Tom (CTO), Colin (CHO). **The "RoofHero Technologies" lead in the brief is a false trail** — that is an unrelated US roof-estimate lead-gen company with its own Trustpilot listing ([trustpilot.com/review/roofhero.com](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/roofhero.com)); no connection to Roofmatch was found.

### Q1b — The fee benchmark Roofmatch is priced against

- **Market average, Feb 2026** (Krib.nl dataset of ~23,000 quotes, reported by Vastgoed Actueel): buyer's agent **EUR 4,400 average, up 13% YoY**; seller's agent EUR 5,800, up 10%. NVM seller's agents run about EUR 500 above Vastgoed Nederland/independent agents; no separate VBO or Vastgoedpro breakout is published ([vastgoedactueel.nl](https://vastgoedactueel.nl/makelaarstarieven-met-10-omhoog/)).
- **Fixed fees** typically EUR 1,500-5,000 excl. BTW, most commonly ~EUR 2,500-3,000; Randstad EUR 4,000-6,000, provincial from ~EUR 2,000. **Courtage** ~1-2% incl. BTW; sources disagree on the NVM band (0.8-1.4% vs 0.5-1.25%), so the precise percentage is unverified. Opstartkosten of EUR 250-500 are cited where present, and charging intrekkingskosten is held to be incompatible with genuine no-cure-no-pay ([nikidewolff.nl](https://nikidewolff.nl/blog/doorstromers/aankoopmakelaar-kosten)) — *a makelaar blog, single-source for the fixed/percentage ranges.*
- **Walter Living** is the closest priced rival and the clearest ladder ([walterliving.com/nl/en/pricing](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/pricing), [their own cost explainer](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/blog/wat-kost-de-walter-aankoopservice/)): free 14-day tier; **Plus EUR 29/month** (unlimited valuation reports, asking-price analysis, sold-price data); **Sessies EUR 149** per one-on-one video consult with a buyer's agent; **Alles-in-1 EUR 2,950 base, no-cure-no-pay**, but taxatie ~EUR 850 and a further ~EUR 450 are add-ons (~EUR 4,250 all-in remote) and in-person viewing accompaniment is ~EUR 1,000 more and limited to select cities (~EUR 5,250).
- **Read-across:** Roofmatch Pro at EUR 2,599 sits ~40% under the 2026 market average and just under Walter's EUR 2,950 base; Pro+ at EUR 3,599 still undercuts Walter's realistic all-in. Roofmatch's differentiator against Walter is that negotiation is included at the base tier, not an add-on.
- **Under EUR 1,000: no full aankoop product found.** The nearest is Walter's EUR 149 advice session. A EUR 1,650 remote "aankoopcoach" product surfaced but the provider could not be identified — lookup failed. Makelaarsland is a fixed-fee *seller's* platform and no standalone buyer product was confirmed ([makelaarsland.nl](https://www.makelaarsland.nl/makelaar/optreden-als-verkoop-en-aankoopmakelaar/)). De Hypotheker bundles were not checked.

### Q2 — How the pre-Funda window actually works

- **Roof Radar's own claim, verbatim:** "Op individuele sites van makelaars worden huizen gemiddeld 24 tot 36 uur eerder gepubliceerd dan op Funda. Wij signaleren dat, omdat we al die sites en off-market kanalen in de gaten houden met AI-zoekbots" ([roofradar.app](https://www.roofradar.app/)). Pricing EUR 1 for 14 days then EUR 29/month. **No named feed, CRM, partnership or API is disclosed anywhere on the site** — the stated method is broad monitoring, i.e. scraping.
- **Independently corroborated mechanisms:** a real "pre-marketing fase" where agents publish to their own site and client database first, plus **stille verkoop accounting for roughly 5-10% of transactions** ([woonadviseur.nl](https://www.woonadviseur.nl/blog/zo-vind-je-huizen-die-niet-op-funda-staan)), which also names the non-Funda aggregators (Huispedia, Huislijn, Huizenzoeker, Miljoenhuizen, Pararius).
- **A second, mechanical contributor:** Funda's own publication pipeline takes ~4 hours to process a submitted listing (~30 minutes with direct publishing) and agents can schedule publication up to 24 hours ahead ([funda.nl/voormakelaars, Funda Desk 2.0](https://www.funda.nl/voormakelaars/update/meer-grip-op-je-publicatiemoment-met-funda-desk-2-0/)). That is a processing and scheduling gap, **not** an embargo.
- **No NVM/Funda embargo or "must publish within X hours of the opdracht" rule was found** in any fetched source. Unconfirmed, not disproven.
- **CRM scraping is the plausible but largely unevidenced leg.** No fetched page named Realworks, Kolibri, Skarabee, Reasi, Stucco or Tiara as a source — except **EarlyKeys**, which claims to scan "meer dan 1.500 bronnen, waaronder publieke platforms, makelaars-CRM's en pre-publicatie-aanbod" at EUR 29-49/month ([earlykeys.nl](https://earlykeys.nl/artikelen/woningradar-versus-funda)). **Liberoaankoop** takes the other route, claiming "het netwerk van makelaars waarmee Libero samenwerkt" — direct partnerships — and states agents often offer to a select buyer group before Funda ([liberoaankoop.nl](https://www.liberoaankoop.nl/woningaanbod-eerder-dan-funda)).
- **Bottom line:** the window is real but it is a publishing-practice artifact, not privileged access. Anyone willing to scrape thousands of makelaar sites can reproduce it; nobody in this set demonstrated a licensed early feed.

### Q3 — Competitor or vendor

- **Roofmatch = competitor, with no vendor surface.** `/zakelijk`, `/partners`, `/api`, `/voor-makelaars` and `/affiliate` **all return 404**. No API, white-label, B2B, referral or affiliate programme was found. It occupies exactly the founder's wedge: AI search plus a human agent on no-cure-no-pay.
- **Roof Radar = credible vendor/partner.** [roofradar.app/zakelijk](https://www.roofradar.app/zakelijk) exists and sells a genuine **white-label programme** — alerts, portal and emails carrying the agent's own name, logo and brand colours. No API, data feed or affiliate tier is mentioned and no price is stated; a white-label consumer portal is not the same as a data feed, so an API would have to be negotiated. **No evidence links Roof Radar to Roofmatch** despite the name similarity — a targeted KVK/founder search returned nothing; any link is unverified.
- **Realworks is the most real supply-side integration point.** [realworks.nl/api](https://www.realworks.nl/api) documents a developer portal at developers.realworks.nl with eight APIs (Wonen, Aankoop, Business, Nieuwbouw, Relatie, Agenda, Taken, Facturen). Access is **broker-gated**: the makelaar buys the API connection via the Realworks CRM Marketplace and controls which data is released to a developer they select. No public pricing.
- **Funda offers no evidenced open API and is actively hostile to automation.** funda.nl and funda.nl/voormakelaars both returned bot-challenge pages (lookup failed). A document titled "Aansluitvoorwaarden voor ongebonden intermediairs" exists at [funda.nl/voorwaarden-en-beleid/aansluitvoorwaarden/funda](https://www.funda.nl/voorwaarden-en-beleid/aansluitvoorwaarden/funda/) but its body was unreadable behind the same challenge. Search snippets (not a fetched page, **unverified**) indicate publishing requires a TIARA user agreement and that data goes to certified software partners rather than an open API.
- **Not checked at all:** Kolibri24/Brixxs, Skarabee, Reasi, Tiara, brainbay/NVM data licensing, Huislijn, Huizenzoeker, Jaap, Move.nl, Altum AI, Matrixian, Walter Living as a data vendor. Named gap, not an empty category.

### Q4 — Will makelaars take a bot's viewing request

- **The strongest finding, and it favours the app.** The NVM Erecode annex (in force since July 2022, agreed jointly by NVM, the Ministry of BZK, VBO, Vastgoedpro and Vereniging Eigen Huis), point D: "Indien een woning openbaar wordt geadverteerd, dient iedereen die dat wenst een woning te kunnen bezichtigen"; where viewing capacity is limited, "aanvullende transparante criteria" may be used but "**Enkel het hebben van een aankoopmakelaar mag daarbij geen reden zijn voor selectie**", and no bid may be requested during pre-selection ([NVM Erecode PDF](https://aankoopmakelaaramsterdam.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/nvm-erecode.pdf)). **The code is silent on the channel a request arrives through** — it neither blesses nor bans a bot.
- The Dutch DPA ruled that at the viewing-request stage a makelaar may ask only for contact details, not income or financial documents ([vastgoedactueel.nl](https://vastgoedactueel.nl/makelaar-mag-alleen-contactgegevens-opvragen-voor-bezichting/)) — which limits what any requester, human or automated, can be asked to supply.
- **Overload is normal and agents already filter.** Funda's own trade article calls dozens of requests on a fresh listing "eerder regel dan uitzondering", notes some agents cap viewings and reject the rest, and recommends phone/email pre-screening that explicitly includes asking whether the buyer has considered an aankoopmakelaar ([funda.nl/voormakelaars](https://www.funda.nl/voormakelaars/artikel-makelaar/slim-omgaan-met-te-veel-bezichtigingsaanvragen/)) — note the tension with the Erecode clause above.
- **The booking mechanism is a shareable link, not an API.** Realworks' Bezichtigingsplanner (branded Move.nl, free on Connect & Pro) has the makelaar publish time blocks; candidates receive a URL by email and SMS to self-book, and "the broker can also copy this URL themselves" — nothing on the page restricts who may hold or use it. It auto-ingests responses from Funda and the makelaar's own site, and the FAQ warns that API-built agent websites may fail to create a request record at all ([faq.realworks.nl](https://faq.realworks.nl/article/hoe-werkt-de-bezichtigingsplanner); a separate FAQ covers [linking a registered aankoopmakelaar to a bezichtigingsaccount](https://faq.realworks.nl/article/een-bezichtigingsaccount-aanmaken-voor-een-aankoopmakelaar-via-realworks)). A reseller, Nexxtmove, documents needing an agenda API koppeling, but framed as internal sync only ([nexxtmove.nl](https://nexxtmove.nl/faq/welke-koppelingen-heb-je-nodig-om-gebruik-te-maken-van-de-bezichtigingsplanner/)).
- **Funda's terms are the hard legal edge.** Its Gebruiksvoorwaarden state that without prior written consent users may not "reproduce, copy, modify, distribute, disseminate, reverse engineer, scrape, decompile, frame, data mine, **use to train (generative) artificial intelligence**, or otherwise use/exploit the platform or material" ([funda.nl/voorwaarden-en-beleid/gebruiksvoorwaarden](https://www.funda.nl/voorwaarden-en-beleid/gebruiksvoorwaarden/)). Funda also serves an active anti-bot challenge reading "We houden ons platform graag veilig en spamvrij" — encountered repeatedly by two diggers.
- **The supply side is already automating, which is the opening.** 2026 Dutch proptech vendors (Utomatic, DenkChat, OpenKlauw, CleverTech, Botpress-NL, Virtualworkforce.ai) sell makelaar-facing chatbots and voice agents that book viewings straight into the office's Google Calendar, Outlook or CRM. These are agent-deployed, not buyer-side — but they mean a makelaar-side bot answering a buyer-side bot is already the emerging shape. *Characterised from search results and vendor marketing, not fetched vendor pages — unverified.*
- **No complaint evidence either way.** Targeted searches for makelaar pushback against bulk, scripted or bot-generated viewing requests ("makelaar klaagt scraper bot bezichtigingsaanvragen platform LinkedIn", "vastgoedactueel bezichtigingsaanvragen honderden bot lead generatie platform makelaar", "AI agent bezichtiging aanvragen makelaar bot 2026 geautomatiseerde bezichtigingsaanvraag") returned only vendor marketing, no forum, LinkedIn or trade-press complaint. **This is an evidence gap, not proof that makelaars don't mind.**

## What this means for the build

1. Price anchor: EUR 2,599-3,599 is already the aggressive end of a EUR 4,400-average market; undercutting Roofmatch means competing with Walter's EUR 29/month tier, not with traditional agents.
2. The pre-Funda window is buyable as a *consumer* product and reproducible by scraping, but nobody demonstrated a licensed early feed — plan for scraping risk under Funda's explicit anti-scraping and anti-AI-training terms.
3. Roof Radar's white-label programme is the one concrete partnership door found; Realworks' broker-gated developer API is the one concrete data-and-calendar door, and it requires recruiting makelaars, not bypassing them.
4. The Erecode gives a strong argument that a buyer represented by an automated agent may not be filtered out for that reason alone — but the actual booking path today is a human-shaped shareable URL, and no rule or practice was found that either authorises or forbids a bot using it.

## Open questions

- **Whether Roofmatch's agents hold NVM/VBO/Vastgoedpro registration.** No claim on their site; the member registries were not checked.
- **Which Roofmatch tier includes what after the winning bid** — two of their own pages contradict each other on Pro's end point.
- **Whether a formal NVM/Funda embargo or maximum-time-to-publish rule exists.** Searched, not found, not disproven.
- **Funda's actual contractual position on automated access**, including the "Aansluitvoorwaarden voor ongebonden intermediairs" body text — blocked by bot challenge on every attempt (lookup failed). Only the consumer Gebruiksvoorwaarden clause was recoverable.
- **Whether Realworks' Wonen/Agenda API exposes a writable bezichtigingsverzoek endpoint** or only generic lead import, and what vetting a third-party developer must pass. Undocumented publicly.
- **Kolibri24/Brixxs and Skarabee entirely** — no viewing-planner, API or partner check was run against either.
- **brainbay/NVM data licensing, and the aggregator/data-vendor tier** (Huislijn, Huizenzoeker, Jaap, Move.nl, Altum AI, Matrixian) — not checked; these are the most likely remaining sources of a legitimate feed.
- **Real makelaar sentiment toward buyer-side bots.** Zero direct evidence found in either direction; worth primary research (interviews) rather than more searching.
- **De Hypotheker and other mortgage-advisor bundled aankoopmakelaar offers**, and the identity of the EUR 1,650 remote aankoopcoach — both unresolved.
- Sub-EUR 1,000 full-service aankoop products: searched, none found; the category may simply not exist, but that is not proven.
