# RR — How community tech-event orgs became entities (Slush, AI Engineer, SPC, Encode, On Deck, Startup Grind) and which legal structures fit AI Builders Club's nonprofit club + for-profit Ventures + contributor virtual shares

Question: Wave 2 of a study on the next best move for AI Builders Club (Amsterdam nonprofit, OCE-hosted, ~10.4k members, 9 cities; manifesto: not-for-profit Club with contributor "virtual shares" + "AI Builders Ventures" running paid programs and funding the Club). Lens: TURNING INTO AN ENTITY. Cases: Slush (volunteers → Slush Oy), AI Engineer / Software 3.0 Inc (licensed partner events), South Park Commons (community → fund), Encode Club ($5M seed), On Deck (stall), plus Startup Grind's for-profit/community split and a European nonprofit-meetup spin-out. Questions: (1) who owns each entity, how the volunteer→company move happened, how contributors were treated; (2) AI Engineer partner-event mechanics and revenue split; (3) does SPC's fund pay for the community and how are they kept apart; (4) which Encode programs justified VC and is it still standing; (5) which On Deck lines failed first and why, and the warning for raising VC on a community; (6) which legal structures actually work for "nonprofit club + for-profit company + contributor shares" (stichting + BV, steward ownership, coops, phantom equity), with real examples. Return: cited mechanics per case, a transferable table (move, evidence, prerequisites, risk), a note on the best-supported legal pattern, open rabbit holes.

## Answer

The closest proven template for AI Builders' two-entity design is Slush. A Finnish not-for-profit foundation owns the operating company, and "most of any profits made by Slush go back to the foundation" ([slush.org/faq](https://slush.org/faq)). That company, Startup Sauna Oy, pays "cost-covering" wages on two levels ([Yle 2017](https://yle.fi/a/3-9949957)) while about 1,700 volunteers run the main event ([slush.org/faq](https://slush.org/faq)). The Dutch version of that pattern is a stichting that holds a priority share in a BV and so steers it ([Wijnen Stael](https://www.wijnenstael.nl/nl/updates/welke-rechtsvorm-past-bij-uw-maatschappelijke-onderneming)). It sits within ANBI rules, because an ANBI "mag wél winst maken uit commerciële activiteiten" if the profit serves its main activities ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/bijzondere_regelingen/goede_doelen/algemeen_nut_beogende_instellingen/aan_welke_voorwaarden_moet_een_anbi_voldoen/winstoogmerk_geen_hoofddoel)).

Contributor economics fit best as non-voting profit certificates through a STAK ([business.gov.nl](https://business.gov.nl/running-your-business/legal-forms-and-governance/trust-office-foundation-stak/)). Phantom payouts are taxed as wages ([Van Ree](https://www.vanreeaccountants.nl/nieuws/medewerkersparticipatie-deel-4-stock-appreciation-rights-en-phantom-shares)), and OCE-hosted funds "are not distributed as personal profit" ([OCE docs](https://docs.opencollective.com/oceurope/llms-full.txt)).

The VC-backed cases give two warnings:
- **On Deck.** Its multi-community model "fractured our focus and brand" ([Yahoo/TechCrunch](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/deck-lays-off-third-staff-184051159.html)). It has since shrunk to a non-dilutive one-week fellowship ([joinodf.com](https://joinodf.com/)).
- **Encode.** Its VC story rested on "six and seven-figure recruitment deals" ([TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/17/encode-club-is-minting-new-web3-developers-amid-crypto-talent-war/)) rather than on the community itself.

## Map

### 1. Slush and European spin-outs (Q1)

**Ownership chain**
- "Slush is owned by the Startup Foundation, a Finnish not-for-profit working towards strengthening the Finnish startup ecosystem" ([slush.org/faq](https://slush.org/faq); also [slush.org/about-us](https://slush.org/about-us)).
- Independently corroborated: "Slushia pyörittää Startup-säätiön omistama Startup Sauna Oy" ([Yle 2017](https://yle.fi/a/3-9949957)).
- The foundation "was founded in 2012 by fifty-seven Finnish technology influencers, entrepreneurs and investors" ([startup-saatio.fi/about](https://www.startup-saatio.fi/about)).
- Its start capital was "runsas miljoona euroa" from Sitra, Teknologiateollisuus and 57 private donors ([fi.wikipedia Startup-säätiö](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Startup-s%C3%A4%C3%A4ti%C3%B6)).

**How the volunteer-to-company move happened**
- "Startup Sauna -kasvukiihdyttämöohjelman perusti opiskelijayrittäjyysjärjestö Aaltoes vuonna 2010. Saunan taustaorganisaationa toimi Startup-säätiö" ([fi.wikipedia Startup Sauna](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Startup_Sauna)).
- "Vuonna 2018 Startup Sauna Oy:n vaihtoi nimekseen Slush Oy" (same source).
- Vesterbacka found a successor in Aaltoes chair Miki Kuusi, and "Syntyi idea, että opiskelijat ottaisivat pääasiallisen vastuun Slushista" ([Yle 2017](https://yle.fi/a/3-9949957)).
- Whether founders ever held Slush Oy shares is not found.

**Profit flow**
- "Most of any profits made by Slush go back to the foundation which uses it to support other ecosystem initiatives" ([slush.org/faq](https://slush.org/faq)).
- The foundation backs "Slush, Junction, The Shortcut, Dash and Maria 01, and as the first home for Wave Ventures" ([startup-saatio.fi/about](https://www.startup-saatio.fi/about)).

**Financials**
- 2016 turnover was "7,4 miljoonaa euroa" and the result "883 000 euroa" ([Yle 2017](https://yle.fi/a/3-9949957)).
- 2024: "Slush Oy teki 11,2 miljoonan liikevaihdolla 1,1 miljoonan euron tuloksen vuonna 2024" ([fi.wikipedia Slush](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slush_(tapahtuma))).

**How contributors were treated**
- Staff: "slushilaiset saavat ('kulut kattavaa') palkkaa kahden tason mukaan, opiskelijat vähemmän kuin valmistuneet" ([Yle 2017](https://yle.fi/a/3-9949957)).
- In 2017: "Varsinaisia työntekijöitä on runsaat kolmekymmentä", and volunteers "yli kaksi tuhatta" (same source).
- Volunteers today: the main event is made possible "with the help of 1,700 volunteers" ([slush.org/faq](https://slush.org/faq)).
- No equity or profit share for volunteers appears in any fetched source. The benefit described is career access: "I actually found my current job at Slush" ([slush.org/audience/volunteers](https://slush.org/audience/volunteers)).
- A 2020 controversy over zero-hour contracts at €15/hour is `unquoted`, taken from a fetch summary of [Yle 2020](https://yle.fi/a/3-11185855).

**Slush'D (licensed local events)**
- From the archived page ([Wayback 2023-12-03](https://web.archive.org/web/20231203072452/https://slush.org/slushd/), quoted by a digger via harvester; UNCHECKED at step 6):
  - "While for-profit entities can qualify as organizers, Slush'D events should not be organized with the intention to turn a profit... we will require that any bottom line be reserved for subsequent events, or that it's reinvested into the local ecosystem."
  - Terms were "all free".
  - Event size was "100–1000 people".
  - The pilot ran in "Trondheim in the fall of 2021".
- Discontinued: "As part of SLUSH's strategic decision to discontinue the Slush'D event series worldwide, this chapter now closes" ([Heilbronn Slush'D](https://www.heilbronnslushd.com/)). No date or reason is given.
- "Myös kansainväliset Slushit ovat voittoa tavoittelemattomia tapahtumia" ([Yle 2017](https://yle.fi/a/3-9949957)).

**European spin-out cases**
- **Junction (Helsinki hackathon):**
  - "Junction Sites are operated by Junction Oy (Business ID 2823785-1)" and "The Junction name and logo are owned by Junction Oy" ([Junction ToS](https://www.hackjunction.com/terms-of-service)).
  - It is "owned by the non-profit foundation Startup Säätiö" and "gathers all of its funding from the partnership contracts between its partner companies" ([en.wikipedia Junction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junction_(hackathon))).
- **Mind the Product:**
  - "Humble beginnings in a London pub — and a journey from just 30 product people to a global community of product managers spanning over 200 cities" ([MTP joins Pendo](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/mind-the-product-joins-pendo/)).
  - Its local ProductTanks are "lead by local organisers, with considerable autonomy" (same source).
  - Whether organizers were paid is not found.
- **Startup Weekend (US-based):**
  - "Techstars... has acquired Up Global, the nonprofit organization that hosts Startup Weekend" ([VentureBeat 2015](https://venturebeat.com/2015/06/16/techstars-acquires-nonprofit-parent-of-startup-weekend)).
  - "In order to acquire a nonprofit, you have to be mission-aligned"; "Terms of the merger weren't disclosed" (same source).

### 3. South Park Commons (Q3)

**Does the fund pay for the community?** Yes, per Forbes 2018 ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexkonrad/2018/04/26/south-park-commons-raises-fund/), quoted by a digger via Wayback; UNCHECKED at step 6):
- "The fund's management fees pay for the community's operating costs, while a portion of the carry in the fund will be kept in trust for the community."
- "members pay $600 for six months to help cover food and event costs, though some have those fees waived."
- "Commons also doesn't take equity to join."
- "A $40 million seed fund... Bankrolled largely by high net worth individuals Sanghvi knows."

**Legal entities**
- Fund III's Form D names "South Park Commons Fund III, L.P." (Delaware), with general partner "SPC GP III, LLC" ([Form D](https://www.streetinsider.com/SEC+Filings/Form+D+South+Park+Commons+Fund/23457473.html)).
- "The Issuer's General Partner (or its designee) is entitled to a management fee" (same source).
- No separate legal entity for the community was found.

**How community and fund are kept apart**
- Two tracks: "Member Residency is open to technologists who... are not yet ready for funding. If you're planning to fundraise soon, we may instead ask you to apply for our Founder Fellowship" ([SPC community](https://www.southparkcommons.com/community/)).
- Fellowship terms: "$400K upfront for 7% on a standard SAFE, plus an additional $600K guaranteed in your next external funding round" ([SPC blog](https://blog.southparkcommons.com/p/spc-founder-fellowship-spring-2026)).
- The ties that remain: "one agreement made early on is to give SPC the right of first refusal to invest", and members "are also invited to invest in SPC funds" ([TechCrunch 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/17/south-park-commons-an-anti-incubator-founded-by-early-fb-and-dropbox-engineers-gains-momentum/)).

**Fund sizes**
- DISPUTED, Fund I: $40M ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexkonrad/2018/04/26/south-park-commons-raises-fund/)) versus $55M ([TechCrunch 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/17/south-park-commons-an-anti-incubator-founded-by-early-fb-and-dropbox-engineers-gains-momentum/), `unquoted`) versus $50M ([SPC press page](https://www.southparkcommons.com/press/), `unquoted`).
- Fund IV is reported at $575M ([Fund Momentum](https://fundmomentum.vc/blog/south-park-commons-fund-iv-575m-minus-one-to-zero-2026)). That source also critiques the shift: "SPC is now underwriting both with the same brand promise."

### 6. Legal structures (Q6)

**Stichting + BV**
- "de stichting een zogeheten prioriteitsaandeel heeft in de BV... krijgt de stichting zeggenschap over de (koers van de) BV" ([Wijnen Stael](https://www.wijnenstael.nl/nl/updates/welke-rechtsvorm-past-bij-uw-maatschappelijke-onderneming)).
- "Er kan zowel financiering worden aangetrokken middels investeerders als via donaties en subsidies" (same source).

**ANBI rules and risks**
- The rule: "Een ANBI mag wél winst maken uit commerciële activiteiten. Voorwaarde is dat de winst ten goede komt aan de hoofdactiviteiten van de ANBI" ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/bijzondere_regelingen/goede_doelen/algemeen_nut_beogende_instellingen/aan_welke_voorwaarden_moet_een_anbi_voldoen/winstoogmerk_geen_hoofddoel)).
- Risk in the wrong direction: a stichting lost ANBI status for structurally supporting its werk-BV, because "het structureel steunen van de bv geen activiteit is die rechtstreeks is gericht op het algemeen belang" ([taxlive 2022](https://www.taxlive.nl/nl/documenten/vn-vandaag/geen-anbi-status-voor-stichting-met-werk-bv/)).
- A second case: a BV's payments to its ANBI owner were non-deductible where the BV was not "(nagenoeg) uitsluitend" raising money for it. "Enig aandeelhouder van X bv is Stichting II. Stichting I is houder van alle certificaten" ([taxlive](https://www.taxlive.nl/nl/documenten/vn-vandaag/uitkeringen-door-bv-aan-anbi-stichting-zijn-niet-aftrekbaar/)).
- Dutch community example: Stichting Zwolse Theaters with Krommer b.v. (a consolidated annual report) is only `unquoted` for the sole-shareholder sentence ([2020-21 report](https://www.zwolsetheaters.nl/media/q42oyp0l/stichtingzwolsetheaters_jaarverslag_2020-2021_getekend_en_gewaarmerkt-1-28.pdf)).

**Vereniging (association) alternative**
- "Alle leden hebben een stem op de ledenvergadering"; "Winst maken mag, maar die moet je gebruiken om het doel van de vereniging te bereiken" ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-vereniging/)).

**STAK and certificates**
- "The STAK splits the following 2 rights of a share: Voting rights... The right to receive a portion of the profit"; "They give the employee a right to a share of the profit, but not the right to vote" ([business.gov.nl](https://business.gov.nl/running-your-business/legal-forms-and-governance/trust-office-foundation-stak/)).
- Certificates "hebben wel 'winstrecht' (economisch eigendom) maar geen 'stemrecht'" ([Archipel](https://www.archipeltaxadvice.nl/insights/steward-ownership-wat-is-het-en-wat-zijn-de-fiscale-gevolgen/)).

**Steward ownership**
- Dutch models: "In the golden share model, the company gives voting shares to stewards. These shares can never be traded" (example Moonback). Under the neutralized-capital model "economic ownership over the company is held by a foundation" (example The Shore). Under the shareholder-foundation model "the entire company... is owned by a foundation" (example Triodos) ([We Are Stewards](https://wearestewards.nl/en/steward-ownership-models/)).
- Purpose veto share: "A non-profit entity holds a veto share, carrying no economic rights but ensuring that the steward ownership principles cannot be overturned" ([Purpose](https://purpose-economy.org/en/purpose-veto-share/)).
- Ecosia "transitioned to steward-ownership in 2018" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steward-ownership)).
- Tony's Chocolonely (light version): "Mission Guardians... hebben geen zeggenschap" ([We Are Stewards](https://wearestewards.nl/tonys-mission-lock-en-steward-ownership/)).

**Cooperatives**
- "Bij een ondernemerscoöperatie verdeel je die winst meestal naar rato van hoeveel elk lid aan de coöperatie heeft geleverd"; "Hoe meer je bijdraagt aan de start-up, des te groter jouw aandeel in de (toekomstige) winst" ([Coöperatie Expert](https://www.cooperatieexpert.nl/artikel/winstverdeling/)).
- Stocksy: "90% of the co-op's surplus profit is distributed back to its artists and paid out in proportion to their contribution"; "over $24.7 million...to their nearly 1,000 artists" in five years ([Start.coop](https://www.start.coop/case-studies/stocksy)).
- Waag: "Waag is a Dutch foundation"; "Waag Futurelab has been officially registered as a Zoöp since May 2025" ([Waag](https://waag.org/en/about-waag/)).

**Phantom equity and virtual shares**
- "De uitkeringen aan de medewerker op grond van SARs of phantom shares vormen in fiscale zin loon" ([Van Ree](https://www.vanreeaccountants.nl/nieuws/medewerkersparticipatie-deel-4-stock-appreciation-rights-en-phantom-shares)).
- "The payout is taxed as regular wage income" ([Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/employee-participation-plans-netherlands/)).
- Winstbewijzen: "Een winstbewijs geeft recht op een of meer toekomstige winstuitkeringen"; for employees "belast als loon in natura" ([Archipel](https://www.archipeltaxadvice.nl/insights/winstbewijzen-behandeling/)).
- Volunteer allowance cap: "Per 1 januari is het maximale bedrag 2.200 euro op jaarbasis"; if exceeded, "betaalt een organisatie belasting over het totaal" ([Fondsenwerving](https://www.fondsenwerving.nl/nieuws/bericht/2026/01/27/onbelaste-vrijwilligersvergoeding-stijgt-met-honderd-euro-naar-2.220-euro)).
- Points systems: "Grain is a form of currency and is meant to be transferable both inside and outside of the project" ([SourceCred](https://sourcecred.io/docs/)). No adoption outcome was found.

**Open Collective Europe (current host)**
- "Funds support the mission; they are not distributed as personal profit."
- "Standard collective hosting is for groups without a separate legal organisation."
- "Donations and sponsorships must be unconditional."
- "Other funding arrangements, including contributions with particular conditions, are reviewed case by case with OCE."

### Note: best-supported legal and ownership pattern for the two-entity, virtual-shares design

The pattern most backed by evidence is a nonprofit (stichting, or a vereniging if members should vote) that owns or controls the commercial company and receives its surplus.
- In practice: Slush ([slush.org/faq](https://slush.org/faq)), Junction ([Junction ToS](https://www.hackjunction.com/terms-of-service)), Mozilla and Wikimedia Enterprise.
- In Dutch law: a stichting priority share in a BV ([Wijnen Stael](https://www.wijnenstael.nl/nl/updates/welke-rechtsvorm-past-bij-uw-maatschappelijke-onderneming)), an ANBI allowed commercial profit that serves its mission ([Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/bijzondere_regelingen/goede_doelen/algemeen_nut_beogende_instellingen/aan_welke_voorwaarden_moet_een_anbi_voldoen/winstoogmerk_geen_hoofddoel)), and steward-ownership models ([We Are Stewards](https://wearestewards.nl/en/steward-ownership-models/)).

Inference, not stated by any source: "virtual shares" carrying money cannot live in the Club itself. OCE says funds "are not distributed as personal profit" ([OCE docs](https://docs.opencollective.com/oceurope/llms-full.txt)), and a vereniging's profit must serve its purpose ([KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-vereniging/)). They fit at the Ventures BV level, as non-voting STAK certificates or winstbewijzen, with the Club or stichting holding the votes or golden share.

None of the event cases gave volunteers equity. Slush pays staff and gives volunteers access ([Yle](https://yle.fi/a/3-9949957); [slush.org/audience/volunteers](https://slush.org/audience/volunteers)). Startup Grind directors are unpaid ([SG BCN](https://www.startupgrind.com/blog/never-stop-grinding-our-honest-letter-to-the-startup-grind-bcn-community/)). Contributor shares would therefore be a novel design, and its tax treatment for unpaid contributors is unverified. Before Ventures exists as a separate entity, the Club needs OCE's agreement: other funding arrangements "are reviewed case by case with OCE" ([OCE docs](https://docs.opencollective.com/oceurope/llms-full.txt)).
