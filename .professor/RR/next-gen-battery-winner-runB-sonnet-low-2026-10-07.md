# RR — Which next-gen battery chemistry replaces lithium-ion at scale by 2030 (EVs, grid, phones)?

Question: What is the next-generation battery that will actually replace lithium-ion at scale — solid-state, sodium-ion, lithium-sulfur, lithium-metal, silicon-anode, or something else? Weigh commercial readiness, energy density, cost, safety and the latest 2025–2026 production evidence against the hype, and name which chemistry wins for EVs, for grid storage, and for phones by 2030.

## Answer

Through 2030, no new chemistry replaces lithium-ion wholesale. In phones, silicon-carbon anodes are the real winner and are already shipping ([Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/galaxy-s27-series-silicon-carbon-batteries-3691129), [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/2188087/android-phones-with-silicon-carbon-batteries/)). In grid storage, sodium-ion is the best-evidenced challenger, but it is projected at only about 10% of storage additions by 2030 ([NextMSC](https://www.nextmsc.com/news/sodium-ion-batteries-enter-commercial-era-with-catls-60-gwh-grid-deal)). In EVs, all-solid-state targets 2027-2028 for first mass production, and only NIO's semi-solid pack ships today ([scorecard](https://liveinthefuture.org/stories/solid-state-battery-scorecard-2026)). That points to lithium-ion EVs with silicon-boosted anodes, plus sodium-ion in low-cost models, by 2030 rather than a solid-state takeover. This verdict is an inference from the evidence below.

## Map

### EVs
- **Semi-solid state.** "NIO is shipping a 150 kWh semi-solid-state battery pack." The ~360 Wh/kg figure has no quote behind it (unquoted). [scorecard](https://liveinthefuture.org/stories/solid-state-battery-scorecard-2026)
- **All-solid-state timelines.**
  - Toyota: "The first vehicles with these cells are targeted for 2027-2028."
  - QuantumScape: "Initial mass production: 2027-2028."
  - Samsung SDI: "Mass production target: 2027."
  - CATL semi-solid: "500 Wh/kg … targeting late 2026 mass production" (unquoted at verify).
  - All from [scorecard](https://liveinthefuture.org/stories/solid-state-battery-scorecard-2026).
- **Lithium-metal (Factorial).**
  - The Mercedes EQS road test "kicked off in February 2025."
  - The cells have "more than 390Wh/kg."
  - "By 2030, Mercedes hopes to have solid-state technology powering a production passenger car."
  - Source: [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/analysis/mercedes-hits-the-road-with-a-prototype-solid-state-eqs/)
- **Silicon anode.** Sila's Moses Lake plant opened in September 2025 and can initially supply material "for 20,000 to 50,000 EVs." It has agreements with Panasonic and Mercedes. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/23/sila-opens-u-s-factory-to-make-silicon-anodes-for-energy-dense-ev-batteries)
- **Sodium-ion in cars.**
  - CATL and Changan unveiled a sodium-ion car in February 2026, "with plans for a mid-2026 market launch."
  - Naxtra delivers "up to 175 Wh/kg."
  - A 45-kWh pack gives "up to 400 kilometers."
  - Source: [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/02/05/catl-sodium-batteries-begin-deployment-in-production-passenger-vehicles/)

### Grid
- **The CATL deal.** CATL agreed "to supply 60 gigawatt-hours (GWh) of sodium-ion batteries to Beijing HyperStrong Technology." That compares with about 9 GWh of sodium-ion shipped in 2025. [NextMSC](https://www.nextmsc.com/news/sodium-ion-batteries-enter-commercial-era-with-catls-60-gwh-grid-deal)
- **Market share.** The IEA, as quoted by NextMSC, projects sodium-ion at "approximately 10% of annual energy storage additions globally by 2030." [NextMSC](https://www.nextmsc.com/news/sodium-ion-batteries-enter-commercial-era-with-catls-60-gwh-grid-deal) This means LFP keeps the grid majority (inference).
- **Cost.** "today's sodium-ion cells are not meaningfully cheaper." [MIT Tech Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/01/12/1129991/sodium-ion-batteries-2026-breakthrough-technology/)
- **BYD.** BYD's sodium-ion ESS "only packs 2.3MWh per 20-foot container, far below the current standard of 5MWh." [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103071860-smm-analysis-byd-launches-sodium-ion-grid-level-energy-storage-system-product)

### Phones
- **Who ships it.** Silicon-carbon cells ship in the OPPO Find X9 Pro (7,500mAh) and OnePlus 15 (7,300mAh). "Samsung has finally embraced silicon-carbon batteries, as its new foldables all have this technology." [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/galaxy-s27-series-silicon-carbon-batteries-3691129) (unchecked)
- **Motorola.** The Razr Ultra (2026) has "a 5,000 mAh Si/C battery." [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/2188087/android-phones-with-silicon-carbon-batteries/)
- **Trade-off.** Si/C cells "suffer from faster degradation." [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/galaxy-s27-series-silicon-carbon-batteries-3691129) (unchecked)
- **Solid-state in small devices.** TDK's 1,000 Wh/L CeraCharge material, announced in 2024, targets earbuds and watches, with no mass-production date. [eejournal](https://www.eejournal.com/industry_news/tdk-successfully-developed-a-material-for-solid-state-batteries-with-100-times-higher-energy-density)

### Lithium-sulfur
- **Lyten.**
  - It supplies mainly drones and defense.
  - Its Nevada plant is "planned to start operations in 2027 with an annual capacity of 10 GWh."
  - It expects cell sales from its Swedish plant from H2 2026.
  - Source: [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104037407-us-lytens-dip-buying-acquisition-of-northvolts-european-assets-lithium-sulfur-solid-state-battery-global-layout-smm-anal)
- **Takeaway.** There is no evidence of EV-scale Li-S before 2030.
