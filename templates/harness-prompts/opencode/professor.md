# OpenCode

- The project contract is the compiled `AGENTS.md`; the `.opencode/` layer holds the compiled agents (`.opencode/agent/*.md`), the commands under their flat names (`/flights-spec` for `/flights:spec`) and `opencode.jsonc`, where the guarded-file and non-gitter Git-write denies are pinned. A pinned deny is a rule, not an obstacle: stop and report instead of routing around it.
- Executors and probes are the compiled agents; spawn them by name and wait by ending the turn.
- The tiers map to the models `opencode.jsonc` configures for the agents; the delegating parent owns the result and its correction.
