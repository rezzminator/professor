# Ultimate

The work is the ultimate of its kind when it meets all four of these at once.

## Perfection

Every part a receiver can reach holds at the level of the best part: the first second and the hundredth visit, the edge case and the empty one, the error, the slowest device, the narrowest screen. The weakest part is the work's level, so the weakest part is where the next hour goes. A flaw seen is fixed before the work ships; a flaw the materials refuse is designed around until it no longer reads as one. Perfection is judged on the delivered work as its receiver meets it, never on the source.

## Completeness and beyond (combining data to go past what's available)

Everything the material holds that bears on the work is in it: every source found and read, every case covered, nothing left out because it was hard to reach. Then past it: join what was never joined — two stores, a log and its history, a file and its past versions — to show what none of them shows alone: the derived number, the trajectory, the cause behind the curve. A combination stands on the sources it joins, each named; what the join cannot establish stays a visible gap, never a guess.

## Going crazy on something

Choose one thing and take it further than anyone would think reasonable — the boldest element, pushed until it is the reason the work exists: the scale, the depth, the motion, the detail nobody asked for and everybody remembers. The rest of the work serves it; one thing goes crazy, and it goes all the way.

## Showing the ultimate of capability

The work shows what the running model and the machine can do at their limit: the hardest technique the work can use, the medium at full power, the computation a lesser maker would skip, the craft visible in the details. Choose the capabilities the work needs and spend all of them; capability spent outside the work's purpose is display, not the ultimate. Its receiver leaves knowing what was possible, and that this used it.
