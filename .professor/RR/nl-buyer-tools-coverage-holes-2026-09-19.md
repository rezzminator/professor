# RR — Dutch home-buyer tools: Funda, second-tier portals, non-Realworks CRMs, AI-buyer startups

Question: Close the coverage holes left by an earlier research pass on Dutch home-BUYER tools (the earlier pass never searched these, and its Funda lookups failed at a bot wall). Score each product against a five-stage flow: (1) AI chatbot interviews the buyer and extracts preferences; (2) continuous scan for new listings; (3) per-home research (value, WOZ, energy, neighbourhood); (4) notifies the buyer and asks "book a viewing?"; (5) books the viewing into the makelaar's calendar. Targets: Funda itself; Huispedia, Huislijn, Pararius (koop), Jaap.nl, Move.nl consumer side, Brainbay/NVM consumer tools; makelaar CRMs other than Realworks (Kolibri, Skarabee, Stepping Stones, Brixxs); any NL startup 2024-2026 claiming an "AI aankoopmakelaar", AI woningzoeker, or automated viewing requests for buyers. For each: stages covered, pricing, live/dead, URL. In scope: NL purchase market. Not in scope: Roofmatch, Roof Radar, Walter Living, Rentbird, Zillow, Redfin, rentals, building the app, anything about a code repository.

## Answer

No Dutch product covers the five-stage flow, and the gap is structural rather than accidental: the consumer portals stop at stage 2 (alerts), every stage-5 booking product is sold to the makelaar rather than the buyer, and stage 1 exists only as a lead-capture widget on a single agency's own website. The one confirmed place in the NL market where a buyer picks a real time slot with no human in the loop is the **Realworks Bezichtigingsplanner**, whose booking screen lives on **Move.nl** — free to agencies on Realworks Connect/Pro, and fed automatically by Funda leads — which makes it the integration surface any buyer-side product would have to reach, not a competitor.

## Load-bearing facts

**Funda's viewing request is a contact form, confirmed from a fetched page.** Funda's own "Hoe gaat een bezichtiging in zijn werk?" states: "Via funda is dit heel gemakkelijk; vul het contactformulier in of bel de makelaar op." The page never shows a slot calendar, availability grid or self-service picker — the buyer requests, the makelaar arranges a time by human contact ([funda.nl, retrieved via web.archive.org](https://www.funda.nl/meer-weten/kopen/hoe-gaat-een-bezichtiging-in-zijn-werk/)). The `makelaar-contact/?listing=...&viewingRequest=true` URL pattern surfaced in search results but was never fetched — [snippet-only].

**Funda's terms forbid exactly what an automated buyer agent does.** The Gebruiksvoorwaarden (version dated 2026-01-01, read from an archived snapshot) permit downloading one copy for personal non-commercial use and prohibit, without prior written permission, reproducing, "scrapen", "dataminen", or using the material "voor het trainen van (generatieve) kunstmatige intelligentie". Funda can also suspend a user so they "geen contactaanvragen meer kan insturen" — i.e. the contact-request channel itself is revocable ([Funda Gebruiksvoorwaarden](https://www.funda.nl/voorwaarden-en-beleid/gebruiksvoorwaarden/)). This is a hard constraint, not a technicality, and it sits directly across stages 2 and 5.

**Realworks Bezichtigingsplanner is the one true buyer self-booking path.** Fetched from two Realworks pages: the buyer selects a day and time slot from blocks the agent published ("zelf een bezichtigingsmoment kiezen binnen de beschikbare blokken"); Funda and agency-website responses are picked up automatically once a listing is "Beschikbaar", with no add-on purchase; it is "gratis beschikbaar voor alle kantoren met Realworks Connect en Realworks Pro"; and the buyer receives an email plus SMS with a URL to a booking interface hosted on Move.nl ([realworks.nl/bezichtigingsplanner](https://www.realworks.nl/bezichtigingsplanner), [faq.realworks.nl](https://faq.realworks.nl/article/hoe-werkt-de-bezichtigingsplanner)).

**Move.nl's consumer side is a post-engagement dossier, not a portal.** It carries no search, no saved searches, no alerts and no per-home data; a buyer only gets an account when their makelaar issues one. Its functions are document exchange, biedlogboek, online bidding and identification. Scale, fetched: "ruim twee miljoen Move.nl accounts" and more than 3,600,000 dossiers created in 2025 ([move.nl](https://www.move.nl)).

**Pararius+ is a rental product that happens to sit on a platform carrying koop listings.** Confirmed from the fetched pages and verified independently: €29,95 per month after a 14-day trial for €0.01, cancellable any time; the free saved search sends one daily digest while Plus sends immediate alerts. But every differentiating Plus feature is rental-native — a "Huurprofiel" sharing income and living situation in one click with the "verhuurder" — and the makelaar-verification rules check whether an office "daadwerkelijk huurwoningen van derden aanbiedt voor langdurige verhuur". There is no koop equivalent and no "Koopprofiel" ([Pararius+](https://www.pararius.nl/info/over-parariusplus)). Viewing requests leave via a "Neem contact op" button or phone, with no bookable slots ([Pararius FAQ](https://www.pararius.nl/info/veelgestelde-vragen-woningzoekenden)).

**Jaap.nl is dead and its traffic was not technically migrated.** Mediahuis pulled the plug in January 2024; the Huispedia post confirming it reads "Mediahuis heeft in januari besloten de stekker uit de woningsite Jaap.nl te trekken… Met het stoppen van de woningsite verdwijnt er een directe concurrent van Huispedia." That is competitive-continuity framing — the post does not describe a listing or data handover ([Huispedia blog](https://huispedia.nl/blog/10194/jaap-stopt-met-koopwoningen-jaap-huizen-nu-op-huispedia)).

**Brainbay/NVM is closed to consumers by design.** Fetched: "Als NVM-lid heb je exclusief toegang tot de Brainbay applicaties." The Market Information API admits external parties only "under the right terms", the main term being "the added value the connection offers to NVM's property agents", and it currently runs inside NVM's internal purchasing tool for agents ([brainbay applicaties](https://brainbay.nl/brainbay-applicaties/), [Market Information API](https://brainbay.nl/en/products/api-market-information/)). This matches NVM's own data-delivery rules, read from a cached primary document: bulk delivery is allowed only to NVM members, NVM participations, brainbay-certified office-automation vendors and — under strict conditions — national government and universities, and is explicitly not allowed for commercial parties; any connection needs a signed dataleveringsovereenkomst approved by the Data Governance Board.

**The NVM ethical code already grants what stage 4/5 is trying to buy, and limits how it can be rationed.** The Erecode annex (binding since 1 July 2022, agreed with BZK, VBO, Vastgoedpro and Vereniging Eigen Huis), read from a cached primary document, clause D: "Indien een woning openbaar wordt geadverteerd, dient iedereen die dat wenst een woning te kunnen bezichtigen." Where viewing capacity is limited, additional *transparent* criteria may be used to select buyers, but merely having an aankoopmakelaar may not be one of them, and no bid may be requested during preselection. Clause E requires equal treatment of all candidate buyers.

**The real stage-5 bottleneck is agent-side triage, not the request channel.** A Funda professional-side article notes that dozens of viewing requests for a freshly listed home are "eerder regel dan uitzondering", that some makelaars cap the number of viewings and turn the rest away, and advises agents to automate the selection funnel with a qualification flow (Funda voor makelaars, "Slim omgaan met (te) veel bezichtigingsaanvragen", 2021 — read from cache; exact URL not captured).

**The AI-buyer-agent category in NL is agent-side SaaS, not a consumer product.** AIKOM.io is the only one covering both stage 1 and stage 5 — its chatbot asks budget, location and room count, then offers time slots and books into the makelaar's calendar — but it is deployed on an individual agency's own website and searches only that agency's listings, so it cannot scan the market for a buyer ([aikom.ai/makelaardij](https://www.aikom.ai/makelaardij)). HousApp raised €4.3M, serves 2,200+ makelaars, launched its AI platform in June 2025 and began life as a viewing scheduler, but its site describes no buyer login or cross-agency search ([housapp.com](https://housapp.com/en/), [Rocking Robots](https://www.rockingrobots.nl/ai-platform-voor-makelaars-haalt-e43-miljoen-op/)). AI Makelaardij NL is B2B SaaS answering inbound enquiries from the agent's own site, with no named clients and "elements in development" ([ai-makelaardij.nl](https://www.ai-makelaardij.nl/)).

**Three of the four named non-Realworks CRMs are not what the brief assumed.** `steppingstones.nl` now 301-redirects to `korian.nl/stepping-stones/`, a healthcare company, and no Dutch real-estate product under that name could be found. `brixxs.com` is an AI/low-code workflow vendor with Topdesk and Exact Online case studies and no real-estate content at all — probably a name mismatch in the brief. `skarabee.com` 302-redirects to [zabun.be](https://zabun.be), whose homepage addresses "honderden Belgische professionals in vastgoed" and mentions no viewing or appointment module. Kolibri is live and real, with a "Beheer van bezichtigingen" feature and a consumer portal called Jouwmakelaar.online for document sharing, but neither page says whether the buyer or only agency staff picks the slot; base CRM pricing is €109/month ([kolibri.software/crm](https://kolibri.software/crm/)).
