# RR — Which next-gen battery chemistry replaces lithium-ion at scale by 2030 (EVs, grid, phones)?

Question: What is the next-generation battery that will actually replace lithium-ion at scale — solid-state, sodium-ion, lithium-sulfur, lithium-metal, silicon-anode, or something else? Weigh commercial readiness, energy density, cost, safety and the latest 2025–2026 production evidence against the hype, and name which chemistry wins for EVs, for grid storage, and for phones by 2030.

## Answer

No chemistry displaces lithium-ion by 2030. The real winner is LFP lithium-ion, which already made up [over 55% of EV batteries in 2025](https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/electric-vehicle-batteries) and [around 90% of battery storage deployments](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-energy-review-2026/technology-battery-storage). The only newcomer at real volume is sodium-ion, with [~9 GWh shipped in 2025](https://battery-tech.net/how-sodium-ion-technology-is-disrupting-the-global-battery-market-in-2026/) and a [60 GWh CATL order](https://carnewschina.com/2026/05/30/catl-to-mass-produce-sodium-ion-batteries-in-2026-targets-600-km-range/), which makes it a complement to LFP in grid storage and low-cost EVs. Solid-state cells are [not yet in mass-market cars, with Toyota targeting 2027-2028](https://liveinthefuture.org/stories/solid-state-battery-scorecard-2026). By 2030 that points to LFP, joined by sodium-ion, for grid storage; LFP/NMC for EVs, with solid-state in premium niches only; and silicon-carbon lithium-ion for phones (the phone verdict is unverified).

## Map

### EVs
- LFP's share of EV batteries rose from nearly 50% in 2024 to over 55% in 2025, and EV battery deployment reached 1.2 TWh, up almost 30% ([IEA GEVO 2026](https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/electric-vehicle-batteries)).
- Solid-state: Toyota received production approval in Japan in October 2025 and plans a 10 GWh factory, with first vehicles targeted for 2027-2028. Samsung SDI targets mass production in 2027. QuantumScape has generated zero product revenue since 2010, and PowerCo holds a licence for up to 40 GWh a year ([scorecard 2026](https://liveinthefuture.org/stories/solid-state-battery-scorecard-2026)). The scorecard says NIO's semi-solid cells have shipped since 2024 in the "thousands", a figure it does not source. Fraunhofer ISI says "only a few solid-state battery cells have been commercialized" ([Fraunhofer ISI](https://www.isi.fraunhofer.de/en/blog/themen/batterie-update/feststoffbatterien-elektro-autos-kommerzialisierung-stand-forschung-entwicklung.html)). BYD reiterated small-scale production of sulfide solid-state cells by 2027 (search snippet only, unverified).
- Silicon anode: Sila's Moses Lake plant started operating in autumn 2025 with 2 GWh/yr of initial capacity. Expansion to 250 GWh is a five-year plan, and Mercedes plans to use Sila's anode in the future electric G-Class ([electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/07/22/sila-raises-300-million-to-expand-us-silicon-anode-production/)).
- Lithium-sulfur: Lyten completed its purchase of Northvolt's Swedish assets on February 27, 2026. Its first test batteries are expected in Q3 2026, and its Nevada plant (10 GWh) is planned for 2027. It currently supplies mainly drone and defence markets ([SMM/Metal.com](https://news.metal.com/en/newscontent/104037407-us-lytens-dip-buying-acquisition-of-northvolts-european-assets-lithium-sulfur-solid-state-battery-global-layout-smm-anal)). Lyten has said lithium-sulfur cycle life is not yet sufficient for EVs ([Heatmap 2024](https://heatmap.news/technology/northvolt-lyten-zeta)).
- Lithium-metal: not researched separately (open).

### Grid storage
- Battery storage deployments reached 108 GW in 2025, up 40%, and LFP accounts for about 90% of deployments ([IEA GER 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-energy-review-2026/technology-battery-storage)).
- Stationary storage pack prices fell to $70/kWh in 2025, 45% below 2024. LFP packs averaged $81/kWh against $128/kWh for NMC ([BNEF Dec 2025](https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/)).
- Sodium-ion: about 9 GWh shipped in 2025, up 150%, and CATL's Naxtra cells reach up to 175 Wh/kg ([battery-tech.net](https://battery-tech.net/how-sodium-ion-technology-is-disrupting-the-global-battery-market-in-2026/)). CATL plans mass production in 2026 and holds a 60 GWh order ([CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2026/05/30/catl-to-mass-produce-sodium-ion-batteries-in-2026-targets-600-km-range/)).
- DISPUTED: sodium-ion cost. CATL reports about $19/kWh at volume, which the same page labels a forecast, against LFP cells at about $55-60/kWh ([battery-tech.net](https://battery-tech.net/how-sodium-ion-technology-is-disrupting-the-global-battery-market-in-2026/)). A search snippet put sodium-ion at $85/kWh against $75/kWh for LFP (unverified).
- Iron-air: Form Energy's first commercial deployment (Cambridge, Minnesota) was expected to operate by the end of 2025, and no source confirms that it has ([Great River Energy](https://greatriverenergy.com/what-we-do/cambridge-energy-storage-project)). Form's backlog grew from about 20 GWh to 80 GWh, and its Series G was a down round ([Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/iron-air-us-ldes-startup-form-energy-secures-us750-million-series-g-financing/)).

### Phones
- Silicon-carbon anode cells appear in Honor and Xiaomi phones, and phones of 6000 mAh or more made up 29% of January 2026 sales (Counterpoint). Both claims come from search snippets only and are unverified.
