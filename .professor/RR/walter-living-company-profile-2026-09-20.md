# RR — Walter Living: who runs it, what it sells, where its data comes from, and what law it sits under

Question: Profile Walter Living (the Dutch home-buying data/advice service, walterliving.com): who founded and runs it, funding and ownership, what the product does and what it costs, which data sources it draws on (Kadaster, funda, municipal data, others), its competitors in the Netherlands, and its legal/regulatory exposure (Dutch broker/makelaar rules, scraping and database rights, GDPR). Map the whole knowledge area; name what stayed unresolved.

## Answer

Walter Living is a small, founder-led Amsterdam buying-agent startup (Walter Tech B.V., KvK 73708585, founded 2019 by Steven van Wel and Marcel de Graaf) that sells a EUR 29/month data subscription and a ~EUR 2,950-3,650 no-cure-no-pay aankoopmakelaar service built on a licensed monthly Kadaster transaction feed plus open BAG/WOZ data — roughly 300 purchases and EUR 1.4M revenue a year, which makes it one of the country's twenty largest buying agencies while its operating company still files deeply negative equity. Its real exposure is not makelaar licensing (the title has been free since 2001) but three softer edges: its funda-derived listing data and browser overlay sit in the legal grey zone that funda has litigated before, its Kadaster licence bars profiling and commercial derivative use of personal data, and its marketing claims — a suppressed Trustpilot rating and unsubstantiated savings figures — are the kind of thing Dutch consumer law actually enforces.

### Product and pricing

- **Walter Plus — EUR 29/month, self-service:** unlimited bid advice, nationwide sale-price/Kadaster data, price-change alerts, an AI "Superagent", a CMA per property, bidding history and Excel downloads, no human agent ([pricing](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/pricing), [Plus](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/plus)).
- **Walter All-in-One — EUR 2,950 incl. VAT, no cure no pay:** data-driven bid calculation, offer submission and negotiation by a human agent, unlimited offers, legal agreement review, guidance to key handover ([All-in-One](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/all-in-one)). Add-ons push the real total higher: appraisal EUR 850, inspection EUR 450, in-person viewings +EUR 1,000 — the FAQ's own math reaches EUR 4,250-5,250 ([pricing FAQ](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/faq/wat-kost-de-walter-aankoopservice)).
- **Fee has moved and is inconsistently published:** EUR 2,950 on the current site, EUR 3,650 in the Jan 2025 Quote profile, and a EUR 3,950 figure in a third source; Quote also reports Walter shifted around mid-2023 from selling standalone paid reports to a bundled brokerage fee. Which figure applied when is unresolved.
- **Coverage:** nine markets with agents — Almere, Amsterdam, Den Haag, Haarlem, Hoofddorp, Leiden, Rotterdam, 't Gooi, Utrecht — remote checklist support elsewhere.
- **A free "Betrap de Makelaar" browser extension** overlays price history and listing status onto funda.nl pages ([Walter's funda page](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/funda), [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/nieuws/browserextensie-souffleert-prijzen-huizenkopers)).
- **Marketing claims are thinly substantiated.** The only figure verified on Walter's own site is "users report saving an average of EUR 15,000" — no denominator, sample or method. Published strategy success rates (Play It Safe 56%, Play to Win 85%, Go All In 96%) are backtests on Kadaster data 2015-2019, not live client outcomes ([Walter blog](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/blog/hoe-walter-living-je-helpt-het-winnende-bod-uit-te-brengen-dankzij-data)). The widely repeated "won 175 of its last 500 bids, ~35%" line could not be found on any Walter page or in Quote, Emerce, BNR or FD — it appears to be a search-engine summary artifact and should be treated as unsourced, along with "1-3 attempts", "3 months to purchase" and "<0.5% used the money-back guarantee".

### Data sources

- **Kadaster transactions — licensed.** Walter's own help page states that "dankzij onze samenwerking met het Kadaster" it receives all Dutch transactions monthly, with the app refreshing daily ([Kadaster help](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/help/kadaster)). Kadaster sells this commercially as Koopsominformatie (~EUR 0.45/address), Bedrijfsmatige Transacties (EUR 1.81/transaction) and bulk BRK Levering (EUR 258/1,000 objects/year) ([tarieven](https://www.kadaster.nl/zakelijk/over-ons/financieel/tarieven)).
- **BAG — open data.** Walter's engineering blog describes ingesting Kadaster's raw BAG XML via the open-source NLExtract toolchain into Postgres monthly and indexing ~9.15M addresses in Elasticsearch ([Medium](https://medium.com/walterliving/a-glimpse-behind-the-scenes-find-out-how-walter-living-searches-nine-million-addresses-within-214d32ae26c4)).
- **WOZ values** from the public WOZ-waardeloket; plus CBS, RIVM, the Cultural Heritage Agency (RCE), RVO and KCAF, described as public sources ([data FAQ](https://walterliving.com/nl/en/faq/where-does-walter-living-get-all-its-data)).
- **funda and JAAP listings — consumed, licence unconfirmed.** The same engineering blog says the address-matching system exists to "find addresses of houses for sale, for example on Funda or JAAP", normalising agent-format addresses against BAG. No source states a funda licence or partnership; the extension reads data client-side off the loaded funda page. **Verdict: unresolved — neither a confirmed licence nor a confirmed dispute.**
- NVM/brainbay and EP-Online energy-label data are not named by Walter; unverified.

### Competitors

- **Data/AVM infrastructure:** [Matrixian](https://docs.matrixian.com/waarderen-en-taxeren/woningwaarde-api) and [Calcasa/Altum AI](https://altum.ai/) sell AVM APIs over Kadaster transactions to banks and appraisers; [brainbay](https://brainbay.nl/en/brainbay-for-nvm/), NVM's own subsidiary, runs the Tiara exchange and Midas database that feed funda itself — the incumbent data layer any entrant must license from or route around.
- **Consumer valuation / AI assistant:** funda's free Waardecheck is lead-gen for local agents rather than a true AVM; **Huispedia** is the closest direct rival, launching "Huispedia Intelligence" / "Collectieve AI" in mid-2026 — a crowdsourced behavioural system flagging overbidding and hidden defects across 4M monthly users, freemium, bootstrapped with no VC ([MT/Sprout](https://mtsprout.nl/groei/wereldprimeur-huispedia-collectieve-ai-die-huizenkopers-vooral-tegen-zichzelf-moet-beschermen)).
- **Broker tooling:** HousApp raised EUR 4.3M for an AI assistant used by 2,200+ brokers and acquired rival Friva in early 2026 — consolidation in the adjacent lane. Ockto (data-sharing fintech) and Reasult (enterprise forecasting) are adjacent, not competing.
- **The baseline** remains traditional NVM/Vastgoed Nederland aankoopmakelaars charging ~1-2% of purchase price or a flat fee — the model Walter undercuts on price and speed.
- Pricing for Matrixian/Calcasa APIs, and the existence/shape of Bieden.nl, Kadasterdata.nl, Zoekjehuis, Huislijn and Woonnu, were not confirmed.

### Regulatory exposure

**Makelaar rules — low bar, rising floor.**
- The protected title "beëdigd makelaar" was abolished 1 March 2001; "makelaar" is a free title with no mandatory diploma or state supervision, so Walter faces **no licensing bar** to operating as an aankoopmakelaar ([nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makelaar)). NVM, and since the Jan 2025 VBO+VastgoedPRO merger Vastgoed Nederland, run voluntary private schemes; membership is not required ([Vastgoed Nederland](https://vastgoednederland.nl/fusie-officieel-vbo-en-vastgoedpro-verder-als-vastgoed-nederland/)).
- **But the free title is under active political attack, aimed squarely at Walter's category.** NVM called in Sept 2026 for statutory quality norms binding on all woningmakelaars ([NVM](https://www.nvm.nl/nieuws/2026/nvm-wettelijke-kwaliteitsnormen-nodig-voor-alle-woningmakelaars/)); PVV MP Jeremy Mooiman floated a concept bill for a mandatory professional register ([Vastgoedjournaal](https://vastgoedjournaal.nl/news/74236/pvv-wil-wettelijk-beroepsregister-voor-woningmakelaars)); and the VRO ministry commissioned RIGO's "Inventarisatie ongebonden makelaars" (17 Feb 2026, Kamerstuk 2026D21730), studying precisely the unaffiliated agents Walter's "100% independent" positioning places it among, recommending self-regulation first and a mandatory biedlogboek plus statutory licensing if that fails ([Tweede Kamer](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D21730)). No bill is drafted yet — a multi-year horizon, but a real one.
- **Statutory duties that bind regardless of membership:** the Wft/AFM licence the moment any concrete mortgage advice is given ([AFM](https://www.afm.nl/nl-nl/sector/adviseurs-bemiddelaars-en-gevolmachtigde-agenten/vergunning-nodig)); BW 7:417 lid 4's ban on serving and charging both sides of a consumer property transaction, breach of which forfeits the entire fee; consumer-law fee transparency; and Wwft client due diligence, UBO verification and FIU-Nederland reporting, which apply to real-estate bemiddelaars as designated institutions ([FIU-Nederland leidraad](https://www.fiu-nederland.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/leidraad_wwft_richtl_makelaars_bemiddelaars_en_taxateurs_onroerende_zaken_tz0041z7fd.pdf)). Buyer-only positioning is a compliance asset under 7:417(4); any referral fee from a seller, developer or portal would recreate the "twee heren" problem.

**Scraping and database rights.**
- funda's terms bar scraping, data mining, framing and AI-training use without written consent ([funda gebruiksvoorwaarden](https://www.funda.nl/en/gebruiksvoorwaarden/)).
- Two independent causes of action exist. **Ryanair v PR Aviation** (CJEU C-30/14, 2015) held that a database owner can enforce reuse restrictions purely through terms and conditions even where no database right applies ([Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/website-operators-can-prohibit-screen-scraping-of-unprotected-data-via-terms-and-conditions-says-eu-court-in-ryanair-case)). **Innoweb v Wegener** (CJEU C-202/12, 2013, the Gaspedaal.nl case) held that a dedicated meta search engine repeatedly querying a third-party listings database and re-displaying results is unlawful re-utilisation under the sui generis right — the closest analogue to any product built over funda listings ([SCL](https://www.scl.org/2984-database-right-innoweb-v-wegener-cjeu-judgment/)).
- **Competition law offers no relief and no threat:** the ACM closed its 2022 preliminary investigation into funda's access conditions, prompted by broker platform Gmak, finding "no convincing evidence" of harm; Gmak withdrew ([ACM](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/geen-nader-onderzoek-voorwaarden-funda)).
- Walter's public stance is openly adversarial — it says it wants to "break funda's power" and runs the "Betrap de Makelaar" campaign against funda-listed agents ([BNR](https://www.bnr.nl/podcast/baanbrekende-businessmodellen/10546338/walter-living-wil-de-macht-van-funda-breken)) — which raises rather than lowers the odds of a dispute.

**GDPR / AVG.**
- Kadaster ownership and transaction data is personal data; its publication rests on the Kadasterwet's public-register mandate, and Kadaster **explicitly forbids reuse for direct marketing, charitable purposes and profiling, and bars commercial derivative products without written permission** ([Kadaster, openbaarheid en privacy](https://www.kadaster.nl/over-ons/wet-en-regelgeving/openbaarheid-en-privacy)). Resale into one's own commercial products is otherwise permitted, with attribution, and the reseller bears responsibility for lawful use ([wederverkoop](https://www.kadaster.nl/over-ons/beleid/informatie-over-wederverkoop)). Whether Walter's public address-level sale-price display counts as a prohibited profiling/personal-data derivative is the live unsettled question.
- WOZ values are personal data per the CBP/AP, which resisted blanket publication before the compromise residential-only WOZ-waardeloket opened 1 Oct 2016 ([AP](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/nl/nieuws/cbp-adviseert-over-algehele-openbaarmaking-woz-waarden-woningen)). Bulk provision of WOZ values still requires an AMvB that has not been adopted — so bulk WOZ republication currently lacks a legal basis.
- **The feared general reuse ban does not reach Walter's data.** The Wet implementatie Open data richtlijn (Kamerstuk 36382, Stb. 2024, 164, in force 19 June 2024) excludes personal data from Who-based reuse by default — but Kadaster states plainly that the Who does not apply to personal data in statutory public registers such as its own ([Kadaster on the Who](https://www.kadaster.nl/over-ons/wet-en-regelgeving/wet-hergebruik-van-overheidsinformatie)). Kadaster data runs under the Kadasterwet regime and WOZ under the Wet WOZ, both outside the Who.
- Walter's own privacy policy does not disclose its property-data sourcing or a legal basis for processing address-level valuation data at all, and names US subprocessors without addressing transfer safeguards — a disclosure gap on its core product.

**Consumer and advertising law.**
- Walter's Trustpilot profile carries a live warning: rating **"unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines"**, reason given as "displaying Trustpilot content in a misleading way" — the 545 reviews (80% 5-star) remain visible, only the aggregate TrustScore is suppressed ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/walterliving.com)). The flagged category is content display, not review authenticity. The ACM can fine fake-review and misleading-review violations up to EUR 900,000 per violation ([ACM](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/acm-pakt-handel-nepreviews-en-neplikes-aan)).
- **Litigation and enforcement record:** rechtspraak.nl and the Reclame Code Commissie decision database were searched directly and returned **zero results** for Walter Tech / Walter Living / Hello Walter ([rechtspraak](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/resultaat?zoekterm=Walter%20Tech), [RCC](https://www.reclamecode.nl/uitspraken/uitspraken-zoeken/?q=walter+living)). The AP, ACM and Geschillencommissie case registers were only checked via web search, not their own interfaces — treat those three as not directly searched rather than clean.
